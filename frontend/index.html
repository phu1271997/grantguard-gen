A funder locks the full grant up front, split into milestones. The grantee submits a link as proof for each milestone — a public transcript, a paper, a repository. The contract reads that page from the live web and an AI reviewer judges whether the deliverable was genuinely met before releasing that tranche. No blind disbursement, no grant officer.

Why this dies without GenLayer: a Solidity contract cannot open a transcript, paper, or repo, read it, and decide "was this milestone actually achieved?". GrantGuard's release decision is read from the live web and reasoned by an LLM under validator consensus — on-chain.
